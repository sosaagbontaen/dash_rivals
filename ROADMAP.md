# DOCUMENT 2 — Future Feature Roadmap

> **DO NOT BUILD THE ROADMAP YET.** This document preserves product ideas. The current
> objective remains extremely narrow: **build one phenomenal 100m race** (see
> [BRIEF.md](BRIEF.md)). If the 100m isn't fun, none of this matters.

## Product Vision

Expand the POC into a full track-and-field universe. Long-term differentiator:
**gameplay + realism + spectacle + simulation + sport fandom** — not just another arcade
running game.

Market note (Aug 2026): Speed Stars (39K ratings, 4.8★) already ships 60m/100m/150m/
200m/400m/800m, hurdles, medley relay, customization, ghosts, leaderboards, and cinematic
replays. Those are *existing market features*, not differentiators.

## Phase 1 — Make Sprinting Deep
Events: 60m, 100m, 150m, 200m, 400m, 800m (expansion content, not differentiation).
Mechanics: reaction, block setup, drive phase, acceleration, transition, max velocity,
speed maintenance, fatigue, wind, altitude, track surface, lane effects. Meaningful race
strategy beyond a fixed tapping rhythm.

## Phase 2 — Hurdles
100mH, 110mH, 400mH. Hurdle rhythm, lead-leg selection, stride pattern, clipping,
fatigue. Speed Stars has 110mH/400mH — aim for greater depth, not mere presence.

## Phase 3 — Relays
4×100, 4×200, 4×400, mixed, medley. Repeatedly requested by Speed Stars players (and it
now has a medley relay). Opportunity: make relays genuinely interesting — baton
exchanges, exchange-zone strategy, team composition, runner order, anchor-leg drama.

## Phase 4 — Field Events
Jumps: long, triple, high, pole vault. Throws: javelin, shot, discus, hammer. Turns the
game into a **true track & field game**, not just a running game.

## Phase 5 — Championship Mode
Fictional international athletics universe: local meets → nationals → continentals →
worlds → Olympic-style championship. Heats, semis, finals, qualification standards, lane
assignments, advancing by place/time. Track as a **sports career**.

## Phase 6 — Athlete Development
Create a fictional athlete; develop acceleration, top speed, speed endurance, reaction,
technique, stamina, specialization. Training mini-games that stay connected to actual
track performance — not a generic RPG.

## Pillar — Your Athlete (cuts across Phases 6 and 10)

Vision only. One athlete, created and owned by the player, whose shape on the
attribute screen *is* how they run.

- **Create** — gender, build, skin tone, hair, uniform, spikes, accessories, and
  running form (stride, cadence, arm carriage, lean) as parameters, not costume.
- **Grow** — EXP per race (time, place, PB, clean form), levels, attribute points
  spent on a seven-axis radial graph: reaction, drive, top speed, speed endurance,
  technique, power, composure. Class caps.
- **Specialise** — classes (Starter, Driver, Flyer, Closer, Technician) and a skill
  tree whose abilities stay inside the sport: reaction window, second gear at 60m,
  a dip worth more, decel that bites later. The fire off the spikes is the first
  "afterburner".
- **Compete** — servers, ranked matchmaking by skill rating and attribute band,
  seasons, ladders (Phase 10 wearing the athlete you made).

What it takes: materials now (skin, kit); form is already parameters; a modular
body (base mesh + blend shapes, clothing/hair meshes on the same Mixamo skeleton)
next; a sprint clip with a real flight phase from self-filmed video-to-mocap next;
attribute screen/EXP/classes later, on-device; accounts/servers/ranked later.

Visual references (in `docs/refs/`, see the roadmap page): DBZ Budokai 3's
attribute menu (the radial graph is the touchstone), a character-dashboard concept,
a character-select concept.

## Phase 7 — The Simulation Engine
Construct hypothetical races: pick 8 athletes, distance, stadium, weather, wind,
altitude, track, era, attributes → SIMULATE → watch. Taps directly into
MotionAthlete-style appeal. Potential major differentiator.

## Phase 8 — "What If?" Mode
Playable athletics sandbox: +2.0 tailwind for everyone? Altitude? 150m race? 100m vs
200m specialist matchup?

## Phase 9 — Historical / Legendary Athletes
Much later, only via appropriate licensing. Never assumed for the POC.

## Phase 10 — Real Multiplayer
Live 1v1, 8-player races, ghost racing, ranked, tournaments. Ghosts/leaderboards already
exist in market — the opportunity is multiplayer that feels like an actual **track
competition**.

## Phase 11 — Stadiums
Olympic-style, indoor, university, rainy, night meet, small local meet, championship
stadium. (Players of competitors specifically request more venues/indoor.)

## Phase 12 — Spectator / Broadcast Mode
Watch races, switch cameras, splits, comparisons, replays, cinematic replay creation —
**a virtual athletics broadcast**.

## Phase 13 — Shareable Race Videos
Post-race GENERATE REPLAY → short-form-friendly clip (cinematic cameras, "9.87 NEW WORLD
RECORD" overlays). Natural TikTok/Shorts growth loop: play → spectacular race → share →
discover → play.

## Phase 14 — Community / Creator Tools
Custom races, tournaments, challenges, shareable simulations, community leaderboards,
creator codes, challenge links ("Can you beat my 100m?").
